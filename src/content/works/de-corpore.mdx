The plan was always three books: bodies, then man, then the citizen. The
citizen came out first, in 1642, because the country was on fire; the
foundation appeared thirteen years later, when the conclusion built on it was
already notorious. Reading the trilogy in the order it was written means
reading it backwards.

### What is in it

Four parts. Logic and method first, where reasoning is defined as computation
with names and science is knowledge of consequences — the doctrine compressed
into [Chapter 5](/chapters/05-of-reason-and-science/). Then first philosophy:
body, space, time, cause, all defined so that nothing incorporeal can be
smuggled in. Then geometry and the proportions of motion. Then physics, which
for Hobbes means showing how the motions of bodies could produce the
appearances, including sense itself.

The method is the one thing to take from it. To know a thing is to know how it
is or could be generated; a circle is understood by the motion that draws it,
not by contemplating circularity. That is why the commonwealth in *Leviathan*
is defined by how it is made rather than by what it is for, and why the schools
are convicted in [Chapter 46](/chapters/46-of-darknesse-from-vain-philosophy-and-fabulous-traditions/)
of reasoning from names that name no generation at all.

### The disaster

Chapter 20 claimed a quadrature of the circle. It was wrong.
[John Wallis](/hobbes/contemporaries/) published a demolition within months,
Hobbes revised and republished rather than concede, and the exchange ran for
a quarter of a century, each round costing him more standing than the last.
He was seventy when it started and ninety when it stopped.

The episode is worth knowing for what it shows about the temperament rather
than the mathematics. He had staked everything on the claim that certainty is
available to anyone who begins from definitions and proceeds in order. To
admit that he had followed that procedure and reached a falsehood was to admit
something about the procedure, and he would not do it.
