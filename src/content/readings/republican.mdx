The neo-roman theory of liberty, which Englishmen took from Livy, Sallust and
the Digest, holds that a man is unfree if he lives at the mercy of another's
will — even if that will never touches him. A slave with a kind master is
still a slave, because the kindness could stop. On that view a subject under an
absolute sovereign is unfree by definition, however lightly he is governed,
and that was the case the parliamentarians were making in 1642.

### Hobbes's counter-definition

[Chapter 21](/chapters/21-of-the-liberty-of-subjects/) answers it by
redefining the word:

> Liberty, or FREEDOME, signifieth (properly) the absence of Opposition; (by
> Opposition, I mean externall Impediments of motion;) and may be applyed no
> lesse to Irrational, and Inanimate creatures, than to Rationall.

If freedom is the absence of impediments, then dependence is not
unfreedom, fear does not make an act involuntary, and a subject is free in
whatever the sovereign has not troubled to regulate. The neo-roman claim
becomes unintelligible in a sentence.

Quentin Skinner's contribution is to show that Hobbes worked at this. The
definition changes between *The Elements of Law*, *De Cive* and *Leviathan*,
tightening each time against the objection, and the final version is
constructed to leave the republicans with nothing to say. Chapter 21 is not
Hobbes explaining what liberty is; it is Hobbes taking a weapon away.

### The modern quarrel

Philip Pettit built a contemporary political theory on the side Hobbes
attacked: freedom as non-domination rather than non-interference, which
justifies constitutions, contestation and a good deal of state action that
non-interference cannot. Hobbes appears in that literature as the founder of
the rival view and its most acute defender — the man who understood the
neo-roman case well enough to see that it had to be answered at the level of
the definition.

Pocock supplied the history that made the recovery possible. The result is
that [Chapter 21](/chapters/21-of-the-liberty-of-subjects/) is now read less as
analysis than as the decisive move in a quarrel about words that is still
running.
