> For Reason, in this sense, is nothing but Reckoning (that is, Adding and
> Substracting) of the Consequences of generall names agreed upon, for the
> Marking and Signifying of our thoughts.

### It depends on speech

Because reason is reckoning with names, nothing without
[speech](/concepts/speech/) can reason. That is the claim behind the whole
apparatus of definitions: a reckoning is only as sound as the counters, so a
man who would have true science must begin by settling the meanings of his
words, and one who begins from names that name nothing will reach conclusions
about nothing. Chapter IV's summary is exact — words are wise men's counters,
they do but reckon by them, but they are the money of fools, that value them
by the authority of an Aristotle, a Cicero, or a Thomas.

### Nobody's reason is the measure

Hobbes then closes the door that most of his contemporaries wanted open. Right
reason exists, as arithmetic exists, but no one man's reason and no assembly's
makes the certainty, any more than an account is well cast up because many
men have approved it. When men reason and disagree, there is no natural
umpire, so they must either fall out or set up an arbitrator whose sentence
they agree to take for right reason. That is a small argument in Chapter V and
an enormous one in the book: the sovereign is a solution to the problem that
reasoning alone does not converge.

### And its abuse

Part IV is the negative case. The schools are convicted in
[Chapter 46](/chapters/46-of-darknesse-from-vain-philosophy-and-fabulous-traditions/)
not of holding false opinions so much as of reckoning with names that were
never defined — abstract essences, separated essences, insignificant speech.
Reason misapplied is not merely error but the material of a
[kingdom of darkness](/concepts/kingdom-of-darkness/).
