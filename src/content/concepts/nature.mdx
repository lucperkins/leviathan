Nature is the book's first word, and it arrives already subordinated to a
maker:

> Nature (the art whereby God hath made and governes the world) is by the art
> of man, as in many other things, so in this also imitated, that it can make
> an Artificial Animal.
>
> — Introduction

That parenthesis does quiet work. If nature is itself an art, then nature and
artifice are not opposites but rather the same kind of thing at different
hands, and what God's art made, man's art may imitate — a watch is an
artificial animal, and the Common-wealth an artificial man. The book's
argument runs between the two senses the word carries here: nature as the
workmanship, and nature as the condition men are left in before any
workmanship of their own.

### The condition of meer Nature

Chapter XIII draws that condition out of a single observation: "Nature hath
made men so equall" that the weakest can kill the strongest, and from equal
ability comes equal hope of the same things. Where two men want what both
cannot have, diffidence; from diffidence, invasion; and without a common
power, the war of every man against every man. Hobbes grants how it sounds —
strange "that Nature should thus dissociate, and render men apt to invade,
and destroy one another" — and answers from experience: the locked door, the
armed journey, the chest with a lock in a house with a lock. The phrase later
tradition settled on, the [state of nature](/themes/the-state-of-nature/), is
not the book's own; Hobbes writes of the condition of meer Nature. Nothing in the
condition is unjust, because [justice](/concepts/justice/) names the keeping
of covenants and there are none yet to keep.

### Not a standard

The Schools' nature was a counsellor: each thing had its end, and "natural"
named what a thing ought to be. Hobbes empties the word of counsel. Nature is
matter in [motion](/concepts/motion/), aiming at nothing, and so it commends
nothing: the natural condition is not an innocence lost but rather the
problem to be solved. The [law of nature](/concepts/law-of-nature/) is not a
statute nature publishes but a theorem reason finds about how to get out; the
[right of nature](/concepts/right-of-nature/) is only what remains to a man
where nothing yet binds him.

### What art must make

Nature does supply the exit's materials: passions that incline men to peace —
fear of death, desire of commodious living, hope to obtain it by industry —
and reason to suggest the articles. But the exit itself is manufactured. By
[covenant](/concepts/contract/) men build the
[Common-wealth](/concepts/commonwealth/), the artificial man of the
Introduction, with the [sovereign](/concepts/sovereign/) for its artificial
soul. Peace does not grow; it is made — nature imitated, and in the one
point that matters to men, improved upon.
