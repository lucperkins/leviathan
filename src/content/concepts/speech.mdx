> The generall use of Speech, is to transferre our Mentall Discourse, into
> Verbal; or the Trayne of our Thoughts, into a Trayne of Words.

Two commodities come of it. One is registering the consequences of our
thoughts, which would otherwise slip out of memory; the other is making them
known to one another. The first is what allows science; the second is what
allows society, and both are what allow lying.

### Names are ours

A name is an arbitrary mark, imposed by us and settled by agreement. There is
nothing universal in the world but names, since the things named are every one
of them individual and singular. Truth and falsity are therefore attributes of
speech and not of things: where there is no speech there is neither, and a
world of creatures without language contains no error either.

Definitions follow. Because names are imposed, the remedy for confusion is to
begin by settling them, and Hobbes's method — the
[chain of definitions](/definitions/) that runs from motion to the
commonwealth — is the practice of that rule.

### What speech makes and unmakes

Everything political rests on it. A covenant is words, so
[contract](/concepts/contract/) needs speech; so do law, authority, and the
[person](/concepts/person/) who represents a multitude. But the same faculty
lets men call good evil and evil good, magnify and vilify, and represent to
others what is not so — which is why, in
[Chapter 17](/chapters/17-of-the-causes-generation-and-definition-of-a-common-wealth/),
bees and ants can live sociably without a coercive power and men cannot. The
faculty that makes the commonwealth possible is the one that makes it
necessary.
