[Marsilius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsilius_of_Padua) wrote
[*Defensor Pacis*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensor_pacis) (*The Defender of Peace*) in 1324, had it condemned by John XXII
three years later, and spent the rest of his life at the court of Ludwig of
Bavaria. Its subject is the one thing he thought could destroy a state: a
second authority inside it, claiming to command.

### Where they agree

The conclusions are nearly interchangeable with
[Chapter 42](/chapters/42-of-power-ecclesiasticall/). Coercive jurisdiction
belongs to the civil legislator and to nobody else. Priests may teach,
admonish and administer sacraments; they may not compel, fine, imprison, or
excommunicate on their own authority, because a power to punish that the
commonwealth has not granted is not a power at all. The church is not a
corporation ruled by the clergy but rather the whole body of the faithful.
General councils, not the Pope, decide what is of faith, and even they are
convened by the legislator.

The diagnosis matches too. Marsilius calls the papal claim to plenitude of
power the singular cause of civil discord, the one disease that turns
kingdoms against themselves — which is Hobbes's
[kingdom of darkness](/concepts/kingdom-of-darkness/) with the theology of
the twelfth century instead of the seventeenth.

### Where they part

On where the civil power comes from. Marsilius's legislator is the whole body
of citizens, or its weightier part, and the ruler governs by their commission
and can be corrected by them. That is the republican foundation Hobbes spends
[Chapter 18](/chapters/18-of-the-rights-of-soveraignes-by-institution/)
demolishing: for him a sovereign who can be corrected is not sovereign. The
same anti-clerical conclusion is reached from opposite ends.

### Did Hobbes read him?

Nothing proves it, and he never names him. *Defensor Pacis* was printed in
English in 1535 as part of Henry VIII's case against Rome, so the argument was
in the English air long before Hobbes; whether he took it from the book or
from the atmosphere is not recoverable.
